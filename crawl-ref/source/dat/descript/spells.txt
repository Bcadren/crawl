Alistair's Intoxication spell

Converts a small portion of the brain matter of those around you into alcohol,
confusing all intelligent humanoids within view (although poison-resistant
creatures may resist the effects). The caster may experience vertigo briefly
as a result of making contact with other minds. It is frequently used as an
icebreaker at wizard parties.
%%%%
Animate Dead spell

Reanimates all corpses and skeletons in the vicinity as followers. This magic
is unstable, so eventually those reanimated will crumble to dust. Zombies and
skeletons cannot leave the level they were created on.
%%%%
Animate Skeleton spell

Reanimates a single skeleton as a follower, even if that skeleton is still
encased in flesh. This magic is unstable, so eventually those reanimated will
crumble to dust. Skeletons cannot leave the level they were created on.
%%%%
Pryten's Skeletal Uprising spell

Upon casting the user is filled with a necromantic energy that will travel through 
their feet to the ground as they walk. Upon stepping off of a skeleton or corpse 
the skeleton may animate coming to the aid of the user. This often expends a small 
amount of magical energy as it happens.

You may use the spell's fail rate to determine the odds of a skeletal ally being 
created with the status active and the spellpower to determine the amount of mana 
which will be discharged:

0 at 200 spellpower; 1 at 100; 2 at 0; randomized between those.

Pulls the top item or group of similar items from a distant pile to the floor

caster's position.

Extremely powerful magical items, such as the Orb of Zot, may actively resist
the pull of this spell.

Constructs a lightning spire in a nearby location. It can fire bouncing bolts
of electricity at the caster's foes, but is completely immobile.
%%%%
Summon Mana Viper spell

Summons a violet-scaled serpent whose bite can swiftly drain the magical
reserves of nearly any foe. At higher power, the antimagic properties of the
viper's bite grow even stronger.
%%%%
Summon Minor Demon spell

%%%%
Summon Mushrooms spell

%%%%
Summon Scarabs spell

Calls forth a swarm of death scarabs.
%%%%
Summon Small Mammal spell

Summons a small mammal to the caster's aid.
%%%%
Summon Spectral Orcs spell

Summons between one and three spectral orcs, specialised for melee combat
and equipped with spectral weaponry and armour.
%%%%
Summon Spider Mount spell

Summons a crafy jumping spider directly below the caster as a means of transportation 
and battle companion. While this spell is active, the caster's movement speed 
will be replaced with that of the spider; that is fast speed on land with no swimming 
capability whatsoever. Additionally, the spider will fight once per turn along with 
its rider attacking the same target as the caster. While active, the caster may call 
upon the spider to jump to nearby spaces or ensnare adjacent foes in webbing. Finally, 
as it occupies the same space as the caster many effects intended for its master will 
strike it instead. It is unaffected by any gear you may be wearing; however, many 
charms and divine effects, such as ring of flames may improve its resistances.

in the caster's vicinity, cutting their resilience in half. This damage is
never directly lethal.

%%%%
Throw Icicle spell

Fires a shard of ice. Due to its strong impact and cutting edges, a
significant portion of its damage bypasses cold resistance. The icicle will sometimes
shatter on impact; dealing cold damage in all directions; the chance of it doing so is
higher if it has traveled less distance.
%%%%
Explosive Entropic Shard spell

Fires a shard of entropic crystal. Due to its strong impact and cutting edges, a
significant portion of its damage bypasses elemental resistances. The shard will sometimes
shatter on impact; dealing chaos damage in all directions; the chance of it doing so is
higher if it has traveled less distance.
%%%%
Throw Klown Pie spell

Throws a harmful yet strangely delicious pie that can inflict its victim with a
number of debilitating temporary effects. These include inducing vertigo,
silence, fire vulnerability, stat drain, or an aversion to drinking potions.
The dreaded moon pie can even turn you into a bat, a hog, or an insubstantial
wisp!

Thunderbolt spell

%%%%
Tomb of Doroklohe spell

Entombs the caster within walls of rock. These walls will push away objects in
their way, but their growth is obstructed by the presence of any creature. They
are not permanent, and will expire after a period of time.
%%%%
Tongue Lash spell

The monster whips a single non-adjacent target with their tongue, dealing weak 
damage and pulling the target closer, if it survives. If pulled to melee range, 
the monster will immediately melee the target as part of the cast.

When used by a chameleon, additionally deals branded damage based on the 
chameleon's colour.
%%%%
Tornado spell

Turns the air around the caster into a mighty vortex, doing tremendous damage
to everything caught in it. Those affected will also be swept up into the air and
tossed around. Only the eye of the storm is a safe place, and it follows the
caster. The spell's effectiveness is greatly diminished in closed areas. After
the tornado fades, it cannot be called forth again for a short time.
%%%%
Typhonic Chaos spell

Turns the air around the caster into a swirling vortex of chaos, doing tremendous
damage and randomly buffing and debuffing everything caught in it. Those affected 
will also be swept up into the air and tossed around. Only the eye of the storm 
is a safe place, and it follows the caster. The spell's effectiveness is greatly 
diminished in closed areas. After the typhoon fades, it cannot be called forth 
again for a short time.

Tukima's Dance spell

Animates the weapon of a targeted foe, ripping it from their hands and causing
it to attack them relentlessly. Once the target dies, the weapon will fall to
the ground shortly thereafter.

Ranged weapons and certain powerful named artefacts cannot be affected, and
any abilities that require evocation skill to trigger will not be active.
%%%%
Twisted Resurrection spell

Causes nearby corpses to begin crawling together, merging into horrible
agglomerations of flesh. More and larger corpses result in more powerful
creations.
%%%%
Unstable Fiery Dash spell

Infuses the caster with extremely strong, but uncontrollable energy.  
The caster dashes through random enemies they can see repeatedly until this 
energy runs out; this deals physical and fire damage to those they pass through 
and leaves trails of fire in their wake. Certain hefty monsters, such as 
statues and those with tentacles that extend beyond their base tile cannot 
be dashed through; all others will be pulled back by the vacuum left in 
your wake.
%%%%
Chaos Dash spell

Infuses the caster with extremely strong, but uncontrollable energy.  
The caster dashes through random enemies they can see repeatedly until this 
energy runs out; this deals randomized elemental damage to those they pass through 
and leaves trails of chaos clouds behind in their wake. Certain hefty monsters, 
such as statues and those with tentacles that extend beyond their base tile 
cannot be dashed through; all others will be pulled back by the vacuum left in 
your wake.
%%%%
Upheaval spell

or rubble.
%%%%
Vampiric Draining spell

Steals the life of an adjacent living creature and grants it to the caster.
Life will not be drained in excess of what the caster can capably absorb.
%%%%
Venom Bolt spell

Fires a penetrating bolt of poison. The range of the bolt is decreased by one
for every creature it strikes.
%%%%
Virulence spell

Makes a targeted creature more vulnerable to poison.
%%%%
Volley of Thorns spell

Launches a small hail of sharp thorns.
%%%%
Vortex spell

Turns the air near the caster into a mighty, short-lived vortex, doing
tremendous damage to everyone caught in it. Those affected will also be swept
up into the air and tossed around. Only the eye of the storm is a safe place,
and it follows the caster. After the vortex fades, it cannot be called forth
again for a short time.
%%%%
Wall of Brambles spell

Causes brambles to rapidly grow nearby. They have almost no structural
strength, but the wicked thorns they bear make their removal difficult. Such
brambles are sparse enough that almost any missile can pass through unhindered.
%%%%
Warning Cry spell

%%%%
Waterstrike spell

Causes the water around or under the target to violently swirl, dealing damage.
It can affect targets standing in or flying above water.
%%%%
Wind Blast spell

%%%%
Word of Recall spell

after a short delay.
%%%%
Yara's Violent Unravelling spell

Tears apart the bonds that bind enchantments to the target creature,
transforming the magic into a spray of deadly, mutagenic radiation.
